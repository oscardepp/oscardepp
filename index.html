About Me

I am a quantitative researcher working in Europe and am a recent grad of Northwestern University. My research spans foundation models for robotics, signal processing, and computational fluid methods. I've designed satellite systems and aircraft wings, including projects for NASA and the U.S. State Department. I welcome opportunities to collaborate on interesting research & engineering projects.
